about us
EC family of products, from EC Productions, is all about managing heights. From domestic to industrial to farming, heights have always been a challenge for many jobs. Different jobs required different kinds of ladders, lifts, & hooked poles of varied lengths and strengths. Now, all that is history with EC family of products that change shape and length as you wish for the job.

How is this possible? EC uses latest world-class aluminium welded technology, found only in defence and aerospace equipments, suitably adapted for Indian conditions. EC products are thus unique, and are awaiting patents. EC Productions also undertake custom designs to suit your special requirements. All EC products are maintenance-free and come with life-time warranty.

EC
EC Family is the brainchild of a team of engineers, who worked for over a decade in USA, Europe, & Middle East as senior aluminium welding specialists. During their professional stay in such developed markets, they addressed the diverse needs of their company’s clients, often custom designing solutions. There these engineers had first hand exposure on how aluminium welded designs were being put to use in conquering heights. While still there, they had this inner calling on why should India be missing out on this easier way of living and working with heights. On that calling, they came back to India, and founded EC Productions whose first product, EC Ladder, which went on to become a roaring success. A team of born inventors and innovators, they also spearheaded subsequent products like EC Lift and EC Thotty (hooked pole), all of which met with excellent reception in the market. Apart from innovative designs that meet the needs of thousands of customers perfectly, EC's success is also due to their usage of only the finest quality aluminium. The team also specializes in custom designs using aluminium welding technology. EC Productions is led by Eldhose C, who worked in this field in USA for more than a decade.
